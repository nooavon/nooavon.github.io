I'm most enthusiastic about using data for explaining different phenomena or building predictive models. Explaining different phenomena with econometrical tools, building predictive models from 
are in applied economics and data analytics: using econometric methods and real-world data to answer policy questions, and testing how well statistical and machine learning models hold up in practice.

Research
------
My bachelor's thesis, *Evaluating "Fair Chance" Employment* (2026, grade 5/5), studies whether Ban the Box laws affected the employment of young minority men in the United States. I replicated and extended a published difference-in-differences analysis using American Community Survey microdata and analyzed the results agaist other more recent studies. The full replication package is [on GitHub](https://github.com/nooavon/Evaluating-Fair-Chance-Employment-Replication-Package).
