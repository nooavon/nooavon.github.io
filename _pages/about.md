I am an Economics student at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en) in Espoo, Finland. I am completing my Bachelor of Science in Economics (minor in Finance), with graduation expected in early 2027, and have started a Master of Science in Business Analytics.

My interests are in applied economics and data analytics: using econometric methods and real-world data to answer policy questions, and testing how well statistical and machine learning models hold up in practice.

Research
------
My bachelor's thesis, *Evaluating "Fair Chance" Employment* (2026, grade 5/5), studies whether Ban the Box laws affected the employment of young minority men in the United States. I replicated a published difference-in-differences analysis using American Community Survey microdata and extended the sample from 2014 to 2019. The full replication package is [on GitHub](https://github.com/nooavon/Evaluating-Fair-Chance-Employment-Replication-Package).
