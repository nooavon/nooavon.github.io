I'm an Economics student at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en) in Espoo, Finland. I'm currently on the final stretch with my bachelor's degree in Economics, and have concurrently started taking the first master's-level courses in Business Analytics.

I'm most enthusiastic about using data for explaining different phenomena or building predictive models. Through my studies, I've built a solid toolkit for econometric and predictive analysis, alongside a strong foundation in microeconomics, macroeconomics, and finance.

**I'm looking for a summer 2027 traineeship** in economic analysis, data analytics or forecasting in the Helsinki region. You can reach me at [nooa.vonschoneman@aalto.fi](mailto:nooa.vonschoneman@aalto.fi) or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nooavon).

Selected work
------
- **[Evaluating "Fair Chance" Employment](/portfolio/1-ban-the-box-thesis/)** (bachelor's thesis, 2026, grade 5/5): did Ban the Box laws affect the employment of young minority men in the United States? I replicated and extended a published difference-in-differences analysis using American Community Survey microdata in Stata. The [thesis](https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/items/804e1b9f-8731-41c7-ba2f-de07e0b5ff57) and the full [replication package](https://github.com/nooavon/Evaluating-Fair-Chance-Employment-Replication-Package) are public.
- **[Forecasting Heart Rate in a Run](/portfolio/7-heart-rate-forecasting/)** (2026): can running power help predict where heart rate is heading? I compared naïve, AR/ARX and Kalman-filter state-space forecasts 1–120 seconds ahead, evaluated fully out of sample in Python.
- **[Predicting a Heavy-Tailed Income Density](/portfolio/2-heavy-tailed-income-density/)** (2026, grade 10/10): how much data does it take to learn the top of an income distribution? I compared spline regression, k-nearest neighbours and gradient boosting on simulated incomes in Python.

Other projects include a flashcard web app built on a PostgreSQL database I designed from scratch, a sustainable fund case study for a pension investor, and a small-sample regression exercise. See the [Projects](/projects/) page for all of them.
